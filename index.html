FAQ

How do I shoot a stack?

Keep the camera as still as possible, ideally on a tripod. Take a series of photos of the same scene, moving the focus a little each time, from the nearest point to the furthest. Many cameras can do this automatically (often called focus bracketing).

Max Detail or Natural?

Max Detail picks the sharpest detail from each photo, down to the finest texture. Best for textured subjects like insects, plants, minerals and fabric. Smooth or dark areas can show a little more grain.

Natural blends the sharp parts of each photo together for a clean, soft result. Best for smooth subjects like products, glass and skin, or dark backgrounds. Very fine detail right next to edges can look slightly soft.

Should I use RAW files?

Yes, if your camera shoots RAW. Fusion Stack develops RAW files itself, which keeps more fine detail than converted exports such as JPEG or PNG.

The photos don't line up. What can I do?

Fusion Stack lines the photos up automatically. Before merging, open the alignment check to step through the photos with an onion-skin overlay, and drag any photo into place. A tripod gives the best results.

Why is my merge smaller than my photos?

On iPhone and iPad with less than 8 GB of memory, merges are made at up to 5000 pixels on the longest side so they fit in memory. On Mac and on devices with 8 GB or more, merges are made at full resolution.

Does the merge keep going if I leave the app?

On iPhone and iPad, yes: the merge keeps running with progress shown by the system, and you get a notification when it's done.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Fusion Stack does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

  • Your photos stay on your device. All merging happens on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. Photos are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Photos library. The app only reads the photos you choose. It asks for permission to add photos so it can save your merged result, and only deletes the original photos if you ask it to after saving.
  • Files. Files and folders you open are only read to make the merge.
  • Notifications. If you allow them, the app shows a local notification when a merge finishes. Nothing is sent over the internet.
  • Settings. Your choices in Settings are stored on your device only.
  • Temporary files. Big merges may use temporary working files on your device. They are deleted when the merge finishes.

The app contains no third-party code that collects data. Because no data is collected, there is nothing to view, export or delete.
